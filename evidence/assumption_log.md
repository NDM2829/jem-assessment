## Step 2 — CSV ingestion and validation

- **Reporting selection:** The default as-of date is the latest valid shift **start** date, and its Monday–Sunday week is the reporting week. An explicit as-of date selects its own week. Rationale: overnight clock-outs and the machine's current date must not move the reporting period. Effect: later hours and predictions will be tied to the selected shift-start week; the loader does not compute either yet.
- **Snapshot labeling:** A selection before Wednesday is an incomplete snapshot. A completed selected week with later data is a historical replay; dates after Wednesday require a Wednesday cutoff in later prediction code. Rationale: these exports do not represent a live, complete Wednesday forecast. Effect: evaluation and prediction displays must use the recorded mode and cutoff, never silently treat later records as Wednesday inputs.
- **Historical availability:** One prior Monday–Sunday shift-date span is the minimum signal for a historical method. If absent, ingestion records `fallback_required`. A date span alone does not prove every worked shift is present. Rationale: records can omit genuine no-work days, so exact day-by-day completeness cannot be inferred. Effect: later prediction code must disclose fallback or missing historical coverage; no accuracy claim follows from the span.
- **Identifiers and joins:** IDs remain literal strings. Blank or repeated IDs stay in raw rows, but repeated keys are excluded from unique lookup indexes, including when duplicate rows are identical. Rationale: selecting one authoritative row without evidence could multiply or misattribute joins. Effect: affected shift hours and notes cannot be safely attributed until corrected; retained raw rows remain available for review. No estimate is treated as observed.
- **Unmatched notes:** Every original note and blank or literal `n/a` is retained. A note gains shift, employee and site attribution only through one unique shift and resolvable employee and site. Rationale: text can still be classified without a valid join. Effect: classifications can include unmatched text, while later site/employee hour attribution must exclude it.
- **Format and missing values:** Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`, times use 24-hour `HH:MM`, numeric hours are finite and nonnegative, and exported breach flags accept true/false, yes/no or 1/0. Invalid row values and missing clock-outs are review warnings, not fabricated values or whole-export failures. Rationale: retain imperfect source evidence. Effect: later hours, predictions and evaluation must exclude or flag affected records under the reference data-quality policy; this step does not calculate outcomes.
- **Auxiliary files:** Employees, shifts and sites are required to process employee hours. Missing or malformed shift notes, holidays or weekly summary disable their dependent outputs without blocking core ingestion. Payroll details are accepted as an unused file and never parsed or retained. Rationale: these sources serve different deliverables and payroll includes sensitive unused values. Effect: missing notes prevent classification, missing holidays remove holiday context, and missing weekly summaries prevent exported-summary comparison; payroll absence or extra fields have no effect on predictions.

## Step 3 — hours, snapshots, and first predictor

- **Weekly accounting and duration:** Each completed shift's full elapsed local wall-clock duration belongs to its shift **start** date and Monday–Sunday week. An end earlier than the start means overnight under the less-than-24-hour assumption; equal start/end and invalid times have unknown duration. No break deduction is available. Rationale: this reproduces the supplied weekly export and both reference notebooks. Effect: hours, clean labels and the >55 target follow this convention; a Sunday overnight is not split into Monday, so calendar-week physical work can differ from the reported week.
- **Strict Wednesday availability:** The predictor snapshot stops at Thursday 00:00 South African local time. A later clock-out is hidden before duration, overlap or estimate calculation; shift dates after Wednesday are excluded. Missing clock-outs and masked later ends are counted separately, though the export lacks creation timestamps and cannot prove when a record was actually available. Rationale: the five-method notebook's strict primary replay supersedes the Stage 1 notebook's shift-date availability assumption. Effect: early hours and probabilities differ from Stage 1's looser snapshot; no future completed duration decides whether a record was ongoing or missing.
- **Estimated duration:** Valid non-overlapping completed shifts from **earlier weeks only** supply personal mean duration, blended with ten peer pseudo-shifts. Peers share role and shift pattern when at least ten eligible shifts exist; otherwise other employees are used. If no peer exists, personal mean is used when available. Unknown records add no observed hours: estimated elapsed is capped by time since clock-in at cutoff, and the balance is overnight carry. An invalid start gets the notebook's 24-hour elapsed cap but remains quality flagged. Rationale: this is the latest notebook's fixed first-version procedure. Effect: estimates support a forecast but never become observed truth or clean outcome labels.
- **Overlaps:** Positive intersections of valid intervals are flagged across sites, midnight and week boundaries; touching intervals are not overlaps. Raw rows and their recorded sum remain, with overlap-affected sums marked suspect. Rationale: keep the reference numeric policy and preserve audit evidence. Effect: no keep-longer or interval-union repair changes hours or predictions; an overlap-affected sum is neither confirmed worked hours nor a confirmed breach.
- **Ambiguous source rows:** Duplicate or blank shift IDs remain in the source and quality counts but contribute neither recorded nor estimated worked hours; their dated employee-week cannot receive a clean label. An undated shift makes that employee's historical labels uncertain across the available period because its week cannot be established. Rationale: counting a duplicate could fabricate hours, while assigning an undated shift to a week would invent evidence. Effect: messy uploads retain reviewable rows and conservative exclusions; the supplied demo has no such ambiguous IDs, so its replay numbers are unchanged. This clarifies the Step 2 unique-ID lookup policy for hours and evaluation.
- **Outcome eligibility:** Full-week recorded totals are built separately from Wednesday features. A historical employee-week is a clean label only when seven shift dates are represented, that employee has a shift, and none of that employee's shifts has missing/invalid duration or an overlap. The selected reporting week is reserved. `weekly_summary` only reconciles exported arithmetic and never supplies features or independent truth. Rationale: match the Stage 1 notebook's conservative label policy. Effect: the replay's measured recall applies only to eligible cases; uncertain cases remain in alert workload and no imputed outcome is promoted to truth. This supersedes Step 2's simplified prior-week date-span indication as a sufficient condition for method support.
- **Register history:** The uploaded employee register's role and shift pattern are treated as static across the supplied historical period, and each registered employee receives a snapshot even with no dated current shift. Rationale: the export contains no effective-dated roster changes and the notebooks use the current register for replay. Effect: historical peer membership and evaluation population can be wrong if employees joined, left or changed roles; absence of current records contributes zero observed hours but does not imply zero final hours or zero risk.
- **Correlated-hours risk:** Personal clean weeks are pooled with role/shift-pattern peer weeks, falling back to all other employees when fewer than ten peer weeks exist. Four peer pseudo-weeks control personal weight. The reference covariance mixture retains negative correlation, floors marginal variance at 1 hour², caps correlation at ±0.95, inflates conditional variance by `1+1/(personal_weeks+4)`, and uses a nonnegative truncated-normal remaining-hours tail. Only completed recorded hours strictly above 55 receive the reference certainty override. Rationale: reproduce the latest notebook without fitting a new model. Effect: every supported registered employee, including new/no-current-record employees, receives a risk; normality and calibration remain unproven and suspect overlap cases retain numeric scores with quality flags.
- **Threshold and comparator:** The naive comparator projects the same Wednesday input hours by `7/3` and alerts only above 55 hours; it is an hours score, not a probability. The initial correlated-hours threshold is fixed at **0.05** in configuration, selected from 1,475 eligible W2–W9 out-of-time forecasts using the 0.01 grid, highest F2, then fewer false alerts, then higher threshold. W4–W9 replay thresholds use only earlier forecasts. Rationale: match the notebook's chronological selection and avoid per-upload retuning. Effect: the supplied six-week replay reproduces 24/44 breaches caught with 137 false alerts for correlated hours versus 23/44 and 260 for naive; future uploads may refresh earlier reference statistics but keep this threshold until explicit offline review changes the version.

## Step 4 — dashboard presentation

- **Site grouping:** The manager queue and its filter group employees by their registered primary site. Shift evidence shows each shift's actual site. Rationale: the register supplies one stable routing site while shifts may occur elsewhere. Effect: site-filter counts describe the employee queue, not worked hours at that site or client identity; predictions and classifications are unchanged.
- **Data-review queue:** A current employee is marked for review when the Wednesday snapshot has an overlap, missing or cutoff-masked clock-out, invalid or ambiguous record, undated record, or no dated current shift. Alert and review groups may overlap, and every registered employee remains visible regardless of score. Rationale: uncertain recorded hours need attention even below the recall-oriented alert threshold. Effect: review counts are operational flags, not extra breaches or clean labels; no observed or estimated hours are changed.

## Step 6 — note causes and historical overtime association

- **Identified client versus site management:** Only an explicit request attributed to a client or `klient` can establish `client_requested`. A centre/site manager's request alone remains `unclear_or_mixed` unless an explicit operational cause is stated. Approval or signature is recorded separately and does not replace a stated operational cause; an explicit client request and independent operational cause without a clear precedence remains unresolved. Rationale: manager identity and approval do not establish who requested extra work or why it occurred. Effect: on the supplied demo, client-associated overtime falls from the notebook's 113.75 to 80.50 hours, unknown rises from 1931.25 to 1964.50, and operational-associated 319.50 and total 2364.50 hours stay the same. Recorded hours and breach predictions do not change. The notebook's manager-as-client rule is **superseded**.
- **Conservative text matching:** The eight reference categories remain. Domain typo correction is limited to unambiguous vocabulary matches of words at least five letters long; original text is never rewritten. Unfamiliar multilingual text that normalises to empty or lacks a specific supported signal stays `unclear_or_mixed` and requires review. Routine placeholders, including literal `n/a`, remain `no_useful_information`. Rationale: unsupported translation or inferred meaning would create unsupported labels. Effect: classifications and review workload can differ from the notebook; predictions and observed hours are unchanged.
- **Multiple notes per shift:** Every source note remains in the classification export, including duplicates and unmatched notes. Only notes linked through a unique shift and resolvable employee/site can attribute hours. Multiple linked notes with different categories make that shift's cause unknown; identical categories count once for the shift. Rationale: a join must not duplicate hours or choose between conflicting accounts. Effect: the unknown attribution bucket may grow, but total allocated hours and prediction inputs are unchanged.
- **Overtime association:** Only clean completed employee-weeks before the selected reporting week enter historical association. Within each, the first 45 recorded hours are ordinary and later hours attach chronologically to their shift's note category and actual site; no-note, routine, unspecified cover and unresolved notes remain in the unknown denominator. Proportional allocation is retained only as an offline sensitivity calculation. Unresolved site IDs are displayed as unknown sites under their original ID. Rationale: no roster or note specifies which exact overtime hours a cause created. Effect: these are associations of existing recorded hours, not causal or billable hours, and excluded weeks/current partial notes do not enter historical totals; breach predictions and clean labels are unchanged.
- **Human review evidence (initial pending status superseded below):** The prepared blind sheet contains a fresh 100-row random sample from notes outside the notebook's 170 AI-reference IDs and a separate 20-row targeted challenge set from the remaining notes. This exclusion slightly narrows the random sampling frame; challenge results are not population estimates. Human labels and reviewers are blank. Metrics require a real reviewer, a taxonomy label and exact matching shift ID and original text; revised rules require another fresh sample for a later independent check. Rationale: AI-reference agreement and reused development examples are not independent human validation. Effect: human accuracy remains pending until review; classification outputs and predictions are unaffected.

## Step 6 — how note sorting was checked and what it found

- **Check:** A human supplied labels for all 100 random notes and 20 separately targeted challenge notes from the blind sheet. All 120 shift IDs and original texts matched the current source, and the rules were frozen at `notes-1.0` before scoring. Category and client/operational/unknown confusion matrices are saved in `analysis/evidence/note_validation_notes-1.0.json`. The sheet has one reviewer and no comments; the review process itself was not independently observed. Rationale: exact matching and frozen rules prevent stale notes or post-review tuning from being reported as the original check. Effect on evaluation: the observed agreement is 93/100 on the random set and 18/20 on the challenge set. The random frame excludes the notebook's 170 AI-reference notes; the targeted set is not a population estimate, and one reviewer is not adjudicated ground truth.
- **Misses and interpretation:** Nine labels disagreed. Five notes saying extra hours were *approved by a client* were labelled `client_requested` by the reviewer but remain `unclear_or_mixed` under the rule requiring an explicit request. Three notes with spelling variants of asked, family and down missed their intended client/absence/equipment categories. One possible personal name (`Clint`) was fuzzy-matched to `client`, producing a client-requested label where the reviewer chose unclear. Rationale: these are substantive ambiguity and matching failures, not evidence to relabel the review. Effect: note classifications and cause-associated overtime can be wrong for similarly worded rows; recorded hours and breach predictions are unchanged. No post-review rule changes were adopted; the exploratory revision was **withdrawn**, and `notes-1.0` remains the reported version.

## Step 7 — record-linked manager actions

- **Remaining-hours allowance:** A numeric allowance to 55 is `max(0, 55 − completed recorded hours at the Wednesday cutoff)` only when the employee has a current dated shift and no missing, masked, invalid, ambiguous, undated or overlapping records in the snapshot. It remains conditional on the export being complete. Otherwise the action says records need confirmation and gives no allowance. Rationale: estimated additions and suspect sums cannot establish a safe numeric remainder. Effect: some breach alerts have no allowance; scores and recorded/estimated hour calculations remain unchanged. A zero allowance does not by itself certify a breach, and the strict target is final hours **greater than** 55.
- **Relief pattern trigger:** Two or more *distinct*, uniquely linked `relief_problem` shifts at the same actual site in one Monday–Sunday shift-start period trigger a review action. Duplicate notes on one shift count once; conflicting note categories do not supply a cause-specific action. Historical periods before the selected week use their recorded notes; selected-week actions stop with Wednesday shift dates. Rationale: repeat reporting can be surfaced without converting a note into a roster fact or misconduct finding. Effect: recommendations and review workload change, while overtime association, note labels, hours and predictions do not.
- **Source-note action scope:** Equipment and explicit client-request checks are generated only for uniquely linked selected-week notes through Wednesday. The original wording and note/shift source rows remain visible. Centre/site-manager approval alone does not create a client request; conflicting categories require review. Absence-cover notes alone do not trigger a misconduct recommendation. Rationale: an action must be traceable to the actual site and wording, without implying repair, authorisation, blame or billing entitlement. Effect: no client-scope action is produced from a manager-only or approval-only note; classifications, predictions and historical association remain unchanged.
- **First-version quality policy retained:** Overlapping intervals keep the existing recorded sum with a suspect flag; missing clock-outs and cutoff-masked ends remain separate, and estimated duration remains separate from observed hours. No keep-longer or interval-union repair, or mean/median replacement, was applied. Rationale: the reference numeric policy remains the first working version while record-level review exposes uncertainty. Effect: forecasts and evaluation remain comparable to the reference, but overlap-affected sums are not confirmed hours or breaches; optional investigations below are not observed truth.

## Step 8 — statistical comparison assumptions (introduced before replay)

- **Past-only usual hours:** For the matched-remainder distance and smoothed-table band, each employee's usual weekly hours are the reference notebook's four-pseudo-week blend of their earlier clean completed weeks and role/shift-pattern peer weeks, with all-other-employee fallback when fewer than ten peer weeks exist. A W1 donor has no earlier usual-hours estimate, so its matched distance omits that term; later donor states use only their own earlier weeks. Rationale: historical donors must represent information available at their own Wednesday, not later outcomes. Effect: predictor scores and evaluation can change from using hindsight states; recorded hours, outcome labels and note classifications do not change.
- **Fixed candidate scores:** Matched remainder uses earlier clean remaining-hour blocks, day mismatch penalty 2, current-hours bandwidth 8 hours, usual-hours bandwidth 5 hours and four peer pseudo-weeks. The smoothed table uses the notebook's usual-hours bands (<=40, 40–45, 45–50, >50), Wednesday days worked, 30 role/pattern pseudo-rows and global prevalence only when no peer group is available. The current week's clean outcome is never a feature, and the completed-recorded-hours >55 certainty override applies to statistical scores only. Rationale: reproduce the shortlisted reference methods without a new model family or parameter search. Effect: these rules determine probabilities, alerts and replay metrics; hours, clean labels and note classifications stay under the existing policy. These scores are estimates, not observed truth or proven calibrated probabilities.

- **Completed Step 8 selection and frozen deployment:** The shared-code W4–W9 replay reproduced the notebook's main TP/FP checks without changing source cleaning: correlated 24/137, smoothed table 31/283, matched remainder 23/177, naive 23/260. The predeclared pooled F2 rule selected correlated hours (F2 0.356 versus naive 0.251). Its threshold was re-derived from 1,475 eligible W2–W9 out-of-time rows available before the original current-week cutoff and remained 0.05. Rationale: select by the declared chronological criterion rather than a retrospective favourite. Effect: the Step 3 designation as an *initial, pre-comparison* choice is superseded by the completed selection, although the numeric method/threshold and this original assessment's prediction bytes are unchanged. Future uploads may refresh only past clean reference statistics under this method; the selected method and threshold stay fixed until versioned offline re-evaluation.
